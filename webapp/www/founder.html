The Story

After years in commercial construction, Mohamed joined the national facilities management sector and in 2019 received a repair request from Abu Auf that sparked a long-term client relationship and a bigger vision for a national company.

He personally answered calls and handled paperwork late into the night before hiring three field technicians and building an office team. With sustained trust from multi-location retailers, Al Azab expanded maintenance and repair across Cairo and Giza, then invested in innovation and sustainability with a digital platform for tracking service requests and real-time updates.

Today, the company is recognized for reliability, quality, and personalized service — while giving back through youth education and environmental initiatives that strengthen teamwork and purpose.
